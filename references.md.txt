# References

## DRAM-inspired synthetic layout

1. Patterson, D. A. and Hennessy, J. L. “DRAM.” CS 294-4 Lecture 2, University of California, Berkeley.  
   Available: https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pattrsn/294/lec2.html  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify the DRAM-inspired row/column organization and the word-line / bit-line representation used by the synthetic layout generator.

2. McDermott, T. “Lecture 14: Memories.” University of Texas at Austin VLSI lecture material.  
   Available: https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~mcdermot/vlsi1/main/lectures/lecture_14.pdf  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify the simplified memory-array view of cells, bit lines, word lines, and sense-amplifier-related array context.

## SEM-inspired imaging and degradation

3. JEOL Ltd. “Edge Effect.” SEM Glossary.  
   Available: https://www.jeol.com/words/semterms/20121024.012800.php  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify gradient-based edge brightening: secondary-electron images can exhibit bright contrast at specimen edges and protrusions.

4. Scanning Electron Microscopy Noise Classification Using Machine Learning. *Microscopy and Microanalysis*, 2025.  
   Available: https://academic.oup.com/mam/article/31/Supplement_1/ozaf048.236/8213140  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify Poisson-like shot noise and Gaussian-like detector/read noise as simplified SEM noise components.

5. Schuller, J. A. et al. “Shot noise-mitigated secondary electron imaging with ion beams.” arXiv:2311.07003.  
   Available: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.07003v2  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify shot-noise modeling in secondary-electron imaging.

## Image registration and matching method

6. Lewis, J. P. “Fast Normalized Cross-Correlation.” Vision Interface, 1995.  
   Available: https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~lowe/425/slides/13-template-matching.pdf  
   Accessed: 18 August 2026.  
   Used to justify normalized cross-correlation for illumination-tolerant template matching.

7. Kuglin, C. D. and Hines, D. C. “The Phase Correlation Image Alignment Method.” Proceedings of the 1975 International Conference on Cybernetics and Society, 1975.  
   Used as background for frequency-domain registration and translation-estimation concepts considered during method development.
